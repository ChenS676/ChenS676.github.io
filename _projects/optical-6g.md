**KIGLIS** was a collaborative project on AI for 6G optical communications with **Nokia Bell Labs** and **VPIphotonics**, funded by the German Federal Ministry of Education and Research (BMFTR), 2022–2024.

I proposed two novel machine learning algorithms for 6G channel equalization and fiber nonlinearity mitigation, presented at the leading venues in optical communications:

- *A Novel Machine Learning-Based Equalizer for a Downstream 100G PAM-4 PON* (OFC 2024, oral, top 5%)
- *Time-Series Forecasting Methods in Fiber Nonlinearity Mitigation* (ECOC 2024, oral, top 5%)
- *Time-Series Prediction Methods in Fiber Nonlinearity Mitigation* (MDPI Photonics 2024, invited)
