As a research intern at the **Max Planck Institute of Biochemistry** (Munich), I work on foundation models for protein design, supervised by Dr. Dexiong Chen and Prof. Karsten Borgwardt.

The focus is on developing and optimizing pre-training algorithms for foundation models applied to molecular property prediction, bringing large-scale representation learning to structural biology.
